The six weeks

Three weeks to close content, then practice tests until test day. And one decision, made this week, that matters more than any of it.

The decision that changes everything

Submit your application this month. Do not wait for the score.

AADSAS lets you submit before your DAT score exists. Your application goes through verification on its own track, and the score attaches when it arrives. Admissions is rolling, so the date your file lands is the date you get in line.

Last cycle your application went in between November and January. If it goes in this month and the score follows in late October, you are roughly two months earlier than you have ever been, on the one axis that actually compounds.

Where you actually are, today

Organic is four banks in of thirteen. Gen chem is two of thirteen. That leaves twenty banks to close in three weeks, which is about seven a week, which is four bank days a week at roughly two banks a day.

The honest arithmetic This is a real load and it is achievable, because the banks left are not evenly hard. Nomenclature and stereochemistry were the slow ones and they are done. Atomic structure, periodic properties and nuclear are fast. Gases, thermodynamics and electrochemistry are the three you named as weak, and they get their own days rather than being squeezed.

If a week slips, the thing that gets cut is biology volume. Never organic.

The rule for a bank

  • Eighty percent is finished. Not one hundred. Get it to eighty and move.
  • Every miss goes in the Ace Log with what actually went wrong, not just the right answer.
  • Check the bank off in the portal when it hits eighty. Your schedule moves itself forward when you do.

The shape of a week

Mornings, which is when you work best. Biology and perceptual ability every single day, short, those are the floor. Gen chem Monday and Friday. Organic Tuesday and Thursday. Wednesday is quantitative reasoning and reading. Saturday is rest and it stays rest through all six weeks. Sunday we meet and build the next week off what you actually missed.

Weeks four to six

Practice tests

Content review teaches you the material. Practice tests teach you the exam. They are two different subjects and you have three weeks for the second one.

Five full lengths, and what each is for

TestWhenWhat you are practising

FL 5Wed Oct 15Light and confident. Sections only, whatever is still shaky.
The rule for this phase The test is worth one day. The review is worth three. Every full length gets a review block longer than the test took, and the review is where the score actually moves.

How a practice test week runs

  • Test day. Sit it. Nothing else that day.
  • The next day. Every miss into the Ace Log, and name what happened: did not know it, misread it, set it up wrong, or ran out of time. The name matters more than the fix.
  • The day after. Work the misses out loud from a blank page. Then back into the bank for whichever topic produced the most of them.
  • Repeat. The gap between tests is not filler. It is the entire point.

Test day, October 12 to 17

  • Book it this week. The centre fills and this is the last variable left open.
  • Nothing new in the last three days. No new banks, no new methods.
  • Scores post within about four weeks and usually sooner, and they go to every school on your list automatically.
Why the date does not need to move Because the application is already in. A score arriving in late October lands in a file that has been verified and sitting in front of schools for a month. That is the entire reason you submit first.
Rest day stays Saturday, except the two Saturdays a full length is scheduled.
