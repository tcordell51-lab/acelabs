Ace Labs runs in two modes. Retold is the learning track — Thomas walks you through a whole subject one night at a time, building everything from a single idea, for when a topic is new or you're rebuilding it. The partial DAT simulation mock.

Sciences (40 Bio + 30 GC + 30 OChem · 90 min) followed by QR (40 Q · 45 min). Question nav panel, mark-for-review, end-of-section review screen, periodic table for Gen Chem, and calculator widget for QR — the closest simulation Ace Labs ships for the sections it currently covers. PAT and Reading Comp are not included; the real DAT places the scheduled 30-minute break after PAT, so this is a high-fidelity two-section simulation rather than a full-length DAT.
