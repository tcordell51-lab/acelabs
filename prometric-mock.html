Test rules (mirrors Prometric center)

  • Timer starts when you click Begin section. It does not pause.
  • Within a section you can navigate freely between questions, mark for review, and skip.
  • Once you submit a section you cannot return to it.
  • The 30-minute break between Sciences and QR is optional — you can skip it.
  • QR section gets a basic 4-function calculator. Sciences sections do not.
  • Closing the tab or refreshing during a section ends the section with whatever you have answered.
