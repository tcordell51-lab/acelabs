# Ace Labs release audit, 2026-10-30

Seven-lens audit of the whole site at HEAD 3a8ed98. Five lenses finished (rules and copy, student flows, function, phone and accessibility, release infrastructure); content accuracy and the Bio engine lens did not run (usage limit). Each finding was meant to get an independent re-check; 21 were verified real before the limit stopped the rest. Unverified does not mean refuted: re-check each before fixing.

Severity: blocker = fix before students; important = fix in the release; polish = small. "Decision" marks Thomas's calls.

## Lens: rules-copy

I audited house rules and copy across all 170 served pages and the JS that injects copy, read-only. Every page was rendered at 390x844 in Playwright and its visible text captured (/tmp/claude-0/-home-user/d13d0ec6-4383-5048-8c7c-ddae9950f2ae/scratchpad/release-audit/copy-house/rendered/), and the visible-text strings in served files were statically scanned with entities decoded (scan.json and all.txt in the same folder). Screenshots, all read: shots/bio-confidence-gate.png, games-play-timer.png, climb-streak.png, gc-home.png and bio-retold-sync.png.

PR #6 fixed the scope estimate and RC lines, the full-length and Game Central counts, the 'Remediation System' titles, the old OChem verdict 'Wrong', 'incorrect' in the diagnostic, and the wrong-answer copy on the six shell pages. Those pages are now clean of em dashes and emoji.

Still not ready for students. The blockers, in order of how soon a new student hits them:

1. **Confidence gates before feedback** in six places:
   - Bio Repair Lab retrieval cards. tools/bio/bio-shared.js:959-993 disables the options and hides all feedback until Guess, Pretty sure or Locked in is tapped. There is no skip, and it covers 177 cards.
   - GC and QR diagnostics: a star rating is required before the reveal.
   - The Climb: 'See the fix' stays locked until a confidence answer is given.
   - Six Views and Chirality Bench: the answers stay disabled until Sure or Shaky is tapped.
2. **Clocks, scores and streaks.**
   - Game Central: a 60-second countdown per question, a live score, a streak and personal bests.
   - Streak counters in The Climb, GC (with 'streak-fire'), QR, OChem, OChem v2 and PAT.
   - Drill stopwatches on the PAT trainers, the GC and QR pacing ladders, Recognition Lab and the Trends drill.
   - The linked A&P review page: a 3-hour countdown plus 148 emoji.
3. **Percentages as grades.**
   - The first-run diagnostic shows '% accuracy' with 'Strong performance' / 'Needs focus' bands.
   - The Prometric results are coloured red, amber and green.
   - Other places have 80% gates and red-under-60% maps.
4. **'Wrong' as a verdict.**
   - A GC miss can read 'What likely happened: Wrong.' (70 such values, plus 164 that say only 'Off.'). QR does the same.
   - The Bio pedigree puzzle shows 'INCORRECT' with a cross.
5. **About 7,800 em dashes and 300 emoji in student-visible text**, including the GC and QR first-run modals, the Retold narration and 70 page titles. The ratchet gate passes all of it: it cannot see &mdash; entities or arrow and shape glyphs, and its baseline tolerates 7,578 dashes and emoji.
6. **False claims.**
   - The Prometric intro says PAT and Reading banks were never built, though both ship.
   - The scope page bank counts are stale under a 'Last verified 2026-10-29' stamp: GC 605 against 627 live, OChem 388 against 1,230, QR 925 against 1,240.
   - A linked 'Guessing math' page converts raw points to scaled scores on the retired 1-30 scale.
   - 'Against the clock you choose' on Practice: the Six Views pace button sets a flag nothing reads.
   - Retold says 'You unlock practice once the story makes sense'. Nothing is locked.
7. **booster-map.html is still served** with Booster in its title and body.
