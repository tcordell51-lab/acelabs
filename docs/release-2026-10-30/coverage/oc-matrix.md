## Answer in one paragraph

Not yet, and the list itself is not the 2026 outline. Of the 82 canonical OC subtopics, **42 are FULL, 33 PARTIAL, 7 THIN and 0 MISSING** under the five-criterion definition. The reaction core is strong: alkenes, alkynes, alcohols, carbonyls, acid derivatives, enolates, EAS, SN1/SN2/E2, acidity, stereo basics, IR and 1H NMR all have a reachable unit (OChem Retold, Roots, Tree, Reaction Maps), an interactive or drawn visual, 8+ exam-register questions with real explanations, and test appearances. The holes are concrete. Seven subtopics are THIN: formal charge (2 DAT-style questions), polarity (0), intermolecular forces (1), functional-group priority (2), nucleophilicity (0, and its trends are never taught), the Lucas test (nothing but a quiz distractor) and Tollens/Fehling (1). The most common failure is criterion 5: 35 subtopics are under-placed on the timed forms or have no item the diagnostic can draw, and 16 of the PARTIAL rows fail only that, because the four full-length mocks were composed by Retold night and put 80 of their 120 OChem items in chemical synthesis, 3 in acid-base and 8 in spectroscopy and lab. Separately, the subtopic list misses what the 2026 ADA outline added or names: combined mechanisms, two-step and multi-step synthesis, reactivity across functional groups, predicting acid-base equilibria and combined spectra have no id, along with eight one-step reaction families (hydrogenation, alkyne additions, alcohol to halide, acid reactions, enolate alkylation and haloform, aromatic side-chain and reduction chemistry, lab separations, amines); together 337 underlying questions in the banks have no canonical home, and the list keeps mass spectrometry, which is in neither the old nor the 2026 outline. And FULL here means the raw material exists. None of the 82 has the five-step lesson that `docs/ace-system-decisions.md` specifies; only 251 of 1698 exam-register OC questions have a detailed explainer that walks the distractors, and none has the visual-first explainer the decisions doc requires.

## How each criterion was measured

- **Inputs.** Every served OChem question record: 3,543 records in 13 sets (OChem engine bank `shared/ochem-bank.js` 1,230; verified 1000-series `games/ochem-bank-1000.js` 1,194; DAT mock pool `scripts/dat-mock-bank.json` 388; 2026-format set `tools/ochem/bank/ochem-2026-pool.js` 203; full mocks `tools/mock/data/full-mocks.js` 120; The Climb `tools/minitests/bank.js` 100; Prometric forms `shared/dat-mock-tests.js` 90; v2 course bank 80; OChem Retold checks 79; trick-video checks 19; mechanism step-throughs 22; name-the-reaction 8; Ace bank 10), loaded in a node `vm` sandbox by `oc-extract.js` (a copy of the census loader). The 25 procedural Tree generators were sampled 300 times each by `oc-gen.mjs`.
- **Classification** (`oc-rules.js`, `oc-classify.js`): one subtopic per record, by weighted keyword cues on stem, key, other choices and explanation, plus a bonus for the source bank's own tag (strict for the 2026 set, whose item types are reliable; weak for the 1000-series, whose group tags are often wrong: its "hybridization-geometry" and "functional-group-id" groups are mostly alkene and product items). Topics the canonical list lacks went to `gap_*` buckets so they stay visible. Copies collapse to one underlying problem: Prometric = pool ids, pool `OC_x` = engine `x` (350 of 388 pool items are DAT-style rewrites of engine items), full-mock items traced to the 1000-series by stem SMILES plus key (114 of 120), exact repeats inside a bank.
- **Classifier accuracy**: 290 items read by hand in seven samples (`scripts/oc-audit-*.txt`, verdicts in `scripts/oc-audit-verdicts.txt`): 258 agree (89%); on the four held-out samples 141 of 160 exact (88%) and 149 of 160 in the same ADA 2026 area (93%). Misses were dual-topic items (an (R) substrate in an SN2 item, an orientation question on a named EAS reaction). Rows are approximate to a few items; area totals are firmer.
- **1 Teaching unit** (`oc-teach.js`, `oc-teach-map.js`, `oc-term.js`, hand mapping in `oc-manual.js`): 217 units were extracted and keyword-scored (OChem Retold 79 scenes; Roots 18; Tree 31 modules plus the 125-reaction table they draw from; Reaction Maps 26 hub and trainer sections, 10 lessons, 8 step-throughs; v2 legacy 10; The Two Decisions; 20 cheat-sheet cards; 13 trick videos; Chirality Bench), then mapped by hand. `yes` = a dedicated unit (a root, a Tree board or reaction row, a Retold scene, or a Reaction Maps hub section with prose and a worked example); `part` = only inside another unit or as reagent lists; `no` = nothing or a mention. Units were spot-read for depth, not proofread line by line.
- **2 Visual or tool**: `yes` = something drawn or manipulable made for it (a Roots drawing, a Tree board, a Reaction Maps trainer or animated mechanism, Chirality Bench, a Retold scene SVG, a trick video; the trick videos are silent today); `part` = only a shared or generic visual.
- **3 Questions**: underlying problems / **DAT-style** problems (5-choice in brackets). DAT-style = the full-mock composer's exam-register rule (`scripts/compose-full-mocks.mjs`: 6+ words, no `x=y` shorthand) plus 4 or 5 choices, and **not** an OChem engine template item: the 877 engine-only problems ("Major product of 1-butene + mCPBA ?", choices "Epoxide (syn)", 105 of them with placeholder names like "5-alkene", one-line `why`) pass the word rule but are drills, not exam register, so they are listed in the notes, not counted. `yes` = 8+ DAT-style, `part` = 3 to 7, `no` = under 3. Records by bank: E engine, K 1000-series, P pool, PT Prometric, F full mocks, C Climb, S 2026 set, R Retold, V v2, M step-throughs, I name-the-reaction, TV trick videos, A Ace bank.
- **4 Explanations**: the best exam-register version of each DAT-style problem, census levels: real = 25+ words (short paragraph or detailed) / detailed = also reasons about every distractor / one line or none. `yes` = 8+ real. The detailed count is a lower bound: the census heuristic cannot see structure-only choices.
- **5 Tests**: appearances in the 4 full-length mocks (30 OChem each) and the 3 Prometric forms (30 each), 210 items, against an expected share. The ADA publishes no per-area item counts that could be checked here (ada.org is blocked, see below), so the weights are the repo's own 2026 blueprint in `scripts/compose-tests.js` (Mechanisms 7, Chemical synthesis 9, Acid-base 4, Chemical and physical properties 6, Structural evaluation 4 per 30; The Summit uses 7/9/4/5/5), split equally across the canonical subtopics in each area. `yes` = at least half the expected count and at least one pool item (`diagnostic.html` draws 15 OChem at random from the 388 pool items with no topic stratification, so a subtopic with no pool item can never appear in the diagnostic; the per-sitting expectation is shown). The Climb count is shown for information.
- **Rating**: FULL = all five `yes`. MISSING = no teaching, no visual, no question and no mention. THIN = under 3 DAT-style problems, or teaching that is only a mention with no visual. PARTIAL = everything else.

## Check of the subtopic list against the ADA outline

**Source.** The ADA PDF (`https://www.ada.org/-/media/project/ada-organization/ada/ada-org/files/education/new_organic_chemistry_examinationspecifications.pdf`, "Updated: 08/13/2025", "Anticipated Implementation - April 2026") could not be fetched: `www.ada.org`, `bootcamp.com` and `boosterprep.com` do not resolve from this sandbox (DNS blocked; they would have to be allowed in the session network settings). The OChem section stays at 30 items.

The 2026 outline has five areas (the previous outline had seven: mechanisms; chemical and physical properties of molecules; stereochemistry; nomenclature; individual reactions of the major functional groups and combinations of reactions; acid-base chemistry; aromatics and bonding):

1. **Mechanisms**: curved arrows; single mechanisms; combined mechanisms; reaction coordinate diagrams.
2. **Chemical synthesis**, now organized by synthesis type rather than by functional group: one-step (substitution, elimination, reactions of alkenes and alkynes, alcohols, ethers including epoxides, radical reactions, aromatics, carboxylic acids and derivatives, aldehydes and ketones); two-step and multi-step (snippets disagree on whether "two-step" is a separate line); the repo transcription adds "reactivity across functional groups".
3. **Acid-base chemistry**: ranking acidity and basicity; predicting products and equilibria.
4. **Chemical and physical properties**: spectroscopy (1H NMR, 13C NMR, IR, combined spectra), laboratory techniques, and structure-property links (polarity, intermolecular forces, solubility, melting and boiling points).
5. **Structural evaluation**: nomenclature (functional groups, IUPAC rules); stereochemistry (conformations, isomer relationships, chirality); structure and bonding (aromaticity, hybridization, resonance).

### The 2026 Organic Chemistry change and the list

The ADA moved to the five-area specification for administrations from about April 2026 (OAT from June 2026). Curved-arrow mechanisms (single and combined) and reaction coordinate diagrams became named subtopics, synthesis was regrouped by number of steps, and acid-base gained "predicting products and equilibria"; the ADA calls this a renaming, not a content change. The list reflects it only partly. The 2026-06-02 pass added a "Reaction Mechanisms & Energetics" category (`oc_arrow_pushing`, `oc_reaction_energy`, `oc_thermo_kinetic`) and its header says it was cross-checked against the 2026 update. But it has no combined-mechanism, multi-step synthesis, chemoselectivity, acid-base equilibrium or combined-spectra id; its 12 display categories and the 14 registry topics (`registry.js` lines 89 to 102) still follow the old functional-group structure; no subtopic carries its 2026 area, so nothing can roll practice up to the five areas the score report now uses; and it keeps mass spectrometry. Ace Labs' newest material (the 2026-format set, Roots stages 5 to 7, the Tree, The Summit) is organized by the 2026 outline; the list and the full mocks are not.

Recommended list changes: add `oc_combined_mechanism`, `oc_multistep_synthesis`, `oc_chemoselectivity`, `oc_acid_base_equilibrium`, `oc_combined_spectra`; widen five existing ids to hold the one-step families above; merge the two qualitative-test ids; retire `oc_mass_spec`; link the polarity, IMF and chromatography ids to their GC twins; and add an `ada2026` field to every OC subtopic (the area column in the matrix above is a ready mapping).
