# Coverage matrix: Quantitative Reasoning, Reading Comprehension, Perceptual Ability

Date: 2026-10-30. Scope: the 54 canonical subtopics in `AceDatPortal/src/scheduling/curriculum/subtopics.js` for QR (40), RC (8) and PAT (6), checked against Ace Labs (`/home/user/acelabs`, read only) plus the Sheets in `AceDatPortal/public/canon/sheets/` (linked from Learn). Every count below was computed by the scripts in `coverage/scripts/qrp/` (listed at the end); nothing was counted by eye.

## Bottom line

**Not yet.** Of the 54 subtopics in this section, 5 are FULL, 44 PARTIAL, 5 THIN and 0 MISSING (QR PARTIAL:35, THIN:5; RC FULL:5, PARTIAL:3; PAT PARTIAL:6).

- **QR has breadth but not depth.** 33 of 40 subtopics have a dedicated teaching unit and 31 an interactive tool, but explanations fail almost everywhere: of 1083 distinct DAT-style QR items, 402 (37%) carry a real explanation (25+ words) and 65 carry any visual. The QR engine bank (1,020 records, also copied into the pool, Prometric and games) explains with one line (median 11 words), and 385 further distinct items are flashcard stems ("5/8 = ?"), not DAT-style. Only 3 QR subtopics clear the explanation bar (Number properties (factors, multiples, primes, GCF/LCM); Data interpretation (graphs / tables); SOHCAHTOA (right-triangle trig)), and each fails another criterion. THIN: Sequences & series; Expected value / weighted average; Similar / congruent triangles; Unit circle & special angles; Law of sines / cosines.
- **The QR full-length tests do not match the current outline.** The 4 full mocks and 3 Prometric forms (280 QR items) contain no data-interpretation figure, no quantitative-comparison item and 1 data-sufficiency item, although those are named areas of the current ADA QR outline; the 220-item formats pool that does cover them (60 QC, 60 DS, 60 DI, 40 applied) is used by no test. Meanwhile 27 of 160 full-mock QR items, 19 of 120 Prometric QR items and 20 of 100 Climb QR items are geometry, trigonometry or unit conversion, which the current outline does not name and which the QR engine itself marks off-spec.
- **RC is the strongest section:** 5 of 8 subtopics are FULL (main idea, detail, inference, tone, structure). Vocab in context is taught in one line of one Sheet; the two strategy subtopics (search vs read, timing) are taught well but cannot carry tagged questions.
- **PAT is one fix from FULL in all six types.** Teaching (Retold night + trainer + Playbook + Sheet), tools, 225 generated items per type in 15 fixed tests and 15 per type in each full mock are all in place; the review explanation is a template line (median 4 to 18 words when the key was chosen) plus a trap note, and cube counting has no trap notes at all.
- Neither RC nor PAT appears in `diagnostic.html` (Bio, GC, OChem, QR only); QR appears with 15 of 21 eligible pool items, 12 subtopics among them.

## How each criterion was measured

1. **Teaching unit** (`[Y]` dedicated QR engine or format module, a primary Sheet, or 2+ Retold scenes; `[P]` only a secondary mention in a broader module or one scene; `[N]` none). Correctness was not re-derived line by line; units were read for scope and depth (word counts and worked-example counts are shown). Engine modules marked off-spec are still reachable as optional review.
2. **Visual or interactive tool** (`[Y]` an engine visual with controls (all 36 QR v3 visuals have sliders or buttons, `qr-visuals-summary.json`), a reachable widget listed in `shared/ace-index.js` qrModules, or rendered DI figures; `[P]` only a static Retold or Sheet picture, or a tool built for a neighbouring subtopic; `[N]` none).
3. **DAT-style questions**: distinct items (stem plus sorted choices) across served banks (QR engine, pool extras, Prometric, formats pool, Climb, full mocks, QR diagnostic, Productive Failure, Friday mini-mock, pacing sim, elimination drill, the one live comparator), excluding copies (pool backup, games/qr.json) and prototypes. DAT-style = full-sentence stem (not the full-mock composer's shorthand rule: under 6 words, `x=y`, or ending in `= ?`) and 4+ choices. Retold scene checks, Pattern Library probes and Sheet checks are counted separately as lesson checks. Each QR record was assigned to one subtopic by `qr-classify.js` (bank tag first, content cues within the tag's candidates; method counts in the script output). `[Y]` 8+, `[P]` 3 to 7, `[N]` under 3.
4. **Explanations**: real = 25+ words of explanation (census "short paragraph" or "detailed"); one line = under 25 words; the best explanation among an item's copies is used. `[Y]` 8+ real and 80%+ of DAT-style items; `[P]` some; `[N]` none.
5. **Tests in proportion to weight**: QR count across the 7 full-length forms (4 full mocks x 40 + 3 Prometric x 40 = 280 items) against an expected count from the per-category weights in `tools/qr/research/agent-w1-dat-specs/REPORT.md` (Algebra 9, Numerical 6, Conversions 3, Prob/Stats 4, Geometry 4, Trig 4, Word problems 10 per 40), which is the legacy outline that the list's categories mirror, split evenly within each category. `[Y]` at least half the expected count; `[P]` present but under half, or only in the diagnostic pools; `[N]` absent. Under the current outline the geometry, trigonometry and conversion expectations would be zero; those rows are marked. RC and PAT: present in every full-mock section.

Ratings: FULL = all five `[Y]`; THIN = under 3 DAT-style questions, or no teaching unit and no tool with under 8 questions; MISSING = nothing; otherwise PARTIAL.

## The list against the official outline

Sources: `www.ada.org` does not resolve from this sandbox (as in the census), and `testpreppal.com` was blocked too, so the DAT Candidate Guide PDF could not be opened. What follows rests on search-result excerpts of the 2026 Candidate Guide, the repo's own notes and the code. **Thomas should confirm against the PDF before the QR geometry/trig decision (decisions doc, design default 5).**

**QR.** The 2026 Candidate Guide, as quoted in search excerpts, lists: algebra (equations and expressions, inequalities, exponential notation, absolute value, ratios and proportions, graphical analysis); data analysis, interpretation and sufficiency; quantitative comparison; probability and statistics; applied mathematics (word) problems. 40 items, 45 minutes, on-screen calculator. A search summary reports an ADA staff answer that no QR item is devoted solely to geometry or trigonometry, though items may use those skills; the QR engine says the same in code (`tools/qr/index.html` near `OFFSPEC_SKILLS`: "trigonometry, geometry-only and unit-conversion content left the QR section in the 2015 to 2016 spec change") and keeps those modules out of its scored surfaces. The repo's spec report and the Climb, full mocks, Prometric forms and QR Retold (nights 9 to 11) still follow the older outline (numerical calculations, conversions, geometry, trigonometry).

- In the outline, missing from the list: **Quantitative comparison** (no subtopic; 60 formats items + 4 Friday items, format module F1, Sheet qr-quantitative-comparison) and **data sufficiency** (no subtopic; 60 formats items + 3 engine items; engine module `ds` and format module F2). "Graphical analysis" is only partly covered by `qr_functions` (Functions, graphs & lines), which has no teaching unit of its own.
- In the list, not named in the current outline: Unit conversions (1), Geometry (6), Trigonometry (4) = 11 subtopics. The "Numerical Calculations" skills (fractions, percentages, approximation, scientific notation) are no longer a named area but are used inside every applied item, so they stay useful as supporting skills.
- In the banks but not in the list: Venn / overlapping sets (engine module `sets`, 9 distinct items, 2 DAT-style) and test-taking strategy prompts (17 distinct, from the `untimed`, `skip-ret` and `guess` skills).

**RC.** The outline describes three passages on dental and basic-science topics, 50 items in 60 minutes, testing the ability to read, organize, analyze and remember new information; it names no question types. So the 6 question-type subtopics and 2 strategy subtopics are Ace's own taxonomy, not an outline check. The RC bank uses 8 types; two are folded into list entries here and could be listed for diagnosis: EXCEPT/NOT (71 items, counted under detail) and application (30, counted under inference). "Trap answers" (RC Retold night 8) is taught but not listed.

**PAT.** The outline's six subtests of 15 items each (apertures, view recognition, angle discrimination, paper folding, cube counting, 3D form development; 90 items, 60 minutes) map one to one onto the list (keyhole, top-front-end, angle ranking, hole punching, cube counting, pattern folding), and `tools/pat/test.html` builds them in that order. No gap.

**2026 Organic Chemistry change (outside this section).** Search excerpts agree the ADA revised the OChem specifications around April 2026 (renamed and recombined categories, slightly less weight on individual reactions and structure, more on acid-base and mechanisms; the ADA called it not a major content change; one listing gives five categories: structural evaluation, mechanisms, chemical synthesis, acid-base chemistry, chemical and physical properties). The list's OChem block still uses 12 chapter-style categories (Structure & Bonding ... Spectroscopy & Lab); its header says the 2026-06-02 pass was "cross-checked ... incl. the 2026 OChem mechanism update", so the content was considered but the category structure does not mirror the new outline. The OC matrix owns that check.

## Defects found along the way (reported, not changed)

1. **Full-length QR forms miss the current outline formats**: 0 DI figures, 0 QC and 1 DS item across the 4 full mocks and 3 Prometric forms; the formats pool (`shared/qr-formats-bank.js`, 220 items, all with worked explanations, 60 with rendered figures) feeds no test.
2. **Off-outline content in the scored forms**: geometry, trig and unit-conversion items make up 27/160 full-mock, 19/120 Prometric and 20/100 Climb QR items, while the QR engine treats the same topics as off-spec. One rule is needed across all surfaces.
3. **QR engine `ds` (Data Sufficiency) module teaches slope**: `VISUAL_BY_SKILL.ds` is `slope-rise-run`, `QR_LOCKS.ds` is the slope formula, and 20 of its 23 bank items are "Slope from (a,b) to (c,d)?" items, which also reach Prometric and the pool tagged `ds`.
4. **Placeholder visuals**: the `trig` and `data-interp` engine modules render the word-problem "Translation dictionary" table as their visual (`visualType: 'word-translate'`), so QR has no trig visual and no engine data visual.
5. **PAT pattern-folding trap notes call non-cube solids a cube**: in the 15 fixed tests, 105 of 225 pattern-folding items fold into a pyramid, prism, triangular prism or tetrahedron, and 98 of those show a distractor note such as "This is the mirror image of the folded cube" (135 distractor reviews). Check: `scripts/qrp/pat-pf-check.js`.
6. **PAT cube counting** has no trap tag on any of its 900 distractors, so its review is the same 8-word line whatever was chosen.
7. **Prometric QR**: 106 of 120 QR items show no explanation on review (census), and 47 use flashcard stems.
8. **diagnostic.html** samples QR from 21 eligible pool items (12 subtopics) and has no RC or PAT block; `shared/scope.js` still says 53 questions / 8 QR.
