## How to read a row

- **Rating**: FULL = all five criteria met. PARTIAL = some met. THIN = mentioned only, or under 3 DAT-style questions. MISSING = nothing.
- **C1 Teach** (a teaching unit a student can reach, at DAT depth): YES = a hub node of 500+ words on it, a Lab tab whose own panels carry 400+ words on it, or a node section of 8+ sentences plus a dedicated Retold scene or A&P section; PART = only inside a broader unit; THIN = a mention. Depth was judged from word and sentence counts plus spot reads; correctness was not audited line by line (known defects are named in the row).
- **C2 Visual**: an interactive Lab tab, scrubber, sandbox, stick-layer animation or true-to-life view on the subtopic itself.
- **C3 Qs**: distinct questions tagged to the subtopic across every served Bio bank, as DAT-style / all. DAT-style = full-sentence stem of 8+ words (not a colon fragment), 4+ choices. Copies (games/bio.json, the pool backup) are dropped, Prometric items are merged with their pool originals, exact repeats across banks are merged. YES needs 8+ DAT-style.
- **C4 Explained**: of the DAT-style items, how many have a real explanation (25+ words; census `short` or `detailed`). Shown as explained DAT-style, then for all items: real / one line / none, and how many are detailed with per-choice reasoning. YES needs 8+ explained DAT-style items.
- **C5 Tests**: items in the 4 full-length mocks (tools/mock/data/full-mocks.js, 160 Bio) + the 3 Prometric forms (shared/dat-mock-tests.js, 120 Bio) = 280 test items; "forms" = how many of the 4 full mocks include it; "diag" = items in the diagnostic's Bio draw pool (150 items, 15 drawn per run). Expected = weight x 280, with weights from the five ADA categories (third-party midpoints: Structure & Function 32.5, Cell & Molecular 22.5, Genetics 17.5, Diversity 12.5, Evolution & Ecology 11; ADA publishes no percentages) split evenly inside each category. YES when test items >= half the expected count.
- Question tags were assigned by `scripts/classify-bio.js`: each bank's own tag fixes the family, keyword rules (`scripts/bio-rules.js`) pick the subtopic; a 90-item random spot check read 87 as correct (about 97 percent).

## The subtopic list against the official outline

Sources: the ADA DAT Biology test specifications (updated December 2021, effective January 2022, March 2022 revision) and the 2026 DAT Candidate Guide, read through search-result extracts because www.ada.org (and kaptest.com, prehealthadvising.wp.txstate.edu) do not resolve from this sandbox; www.ada.org has to be allowed in the session network settings to read the PDFs directly. Current official Biology outline (40 items):

- Cell and Molecular Biology: cell metabolism (incl. photosynthesis, enzymology), cellular processes, thermodynamics, mitosis/meiosis, cell structure, experimental cell biology, biomolecules, integrated relationships.
- Diversity of Life: viruses, Archaebacteria, Eubacteria, Fungi, Protista, Plantae, Animalia, integrated relationships.
- Structure and Function of Systems: integumentary, skeletal, muscular, circulatory, lymphatic/immune, digestive, respiratory, urinary, nervous/sensory, endocrine, reproductive, integrated relationships.
- Genetics: molecular, human, classical and chromosomal genetics, genetic technology, developmental mechanisms, genomics, gene expression, epigenetics, integrated relationships.
- Evolution and Ecology: natural selection, population genetics/speciation, animal behavior, ecology (population, community, ecosystem), integrated relationships.
- Removed in 2022: Origin of Life and the separate Developmental Biology category (development now sits under Genetics as "developmental mechanisms" and under the reproductive system).

**Listed topics outside the outline or mislabeled**

- Nothing in BIO_SUBTOPICS is outside the outline. Borderline: `bio_endosymbiosis` is the remnant of the removed Origin of Life topic; keep it as low weight under organelles. `bio_biomes` is in scope (ecosystem ecology) but rarely tested.
- `bio_fermentation` is labelled "Fermentation (anaerobic respiration)". Those are two different processes (anaerobic respiration runs an electron transport chain with a non-oxygen final acceptor); the label teaches a misconception. Rename to "Fermentation".
- Structure: the list still uses the pre-2022 six-category shape (`bio_development_reproduction` topicId, a "Development & Reproduction" category), and puts Hardy-Weinberg under Genetics while the ADA puts population genetics under Evolution and Ecology. The `cat` field is only navigation, but the `topicId` rollups feed the scheduling engine, so the weighting of these rows follows the old structure.
- Not Biology, noted because the task asked: the subtopic list header says it was cross-checked against the 2026 OChem update, and GC/OC rows include `oc_arrow_pushing`, `oc_reaction_energy` and an Acid-Base Chemistry category, which matches what prep sites report about the April 2026 OChem specification (more mechanism and acid-base weight, same 30 items).
