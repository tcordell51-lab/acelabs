06Ranked — and the leverage to get every dimension to 9+

Strongest to weakest, with the single highest-leverage move per engine.

1 · Organic Chem · 8.4

Lift to 9.5: implement predict-the-step on the next 5 mechanisms (SN1, E2, E1 dehydration, imine, aldol). Each addition is structurally identical to the SN2 / EAS / acetal templates already shipped — about a half-day per mechanism. That single change moves both Active-learning depth and Coverage up by full points.

2 · QR · 8.1

Lift to 9.0: promote the pacing-sim Two-Pass Drill from its proposal iframe to the canonical pacing experience on the main page, and ship the actual Calculator Tax toggle (the calcOpens infrastructure already exists in the mini-mock iframe). Together these convert QR's already-strong active-learning depth into a real student-facing default.

3 · Gen Chem · 8.0

Lift to 8.8: wire the 77-entry misconception taxonomy into the bank — add misconceptionIds: [] to each wrong-choice diag on the top 100 most-missed items, then surface "your top 3 misconception clusters" in the post-mock screen. This converts the taxonomy from documentation into a working diagnostic engine and addresses the stub-diag problem in one move. Then build the solubility-rules + net-ionic mini-module (4 items minimum) — closes the biggest DAT coverage gap.

4 · Biology · 7.7

Lift to 8.6: author real q / qh / ex content for the 23 placeholder retrieval nodes in bio-shared.js — restores the "every miss generates a card" promise that's currently silently broken on the busiest Cell / Genetics topics. Then invert retrieval order on Tier-1 nodes so retrieval comes before the rule reveal (or move retrieval to a sibling page so students must page away first). And send 2-3 bio-heavy Zoom sessions so the voice codex can fill in the bio-specific authoring.

The compound effect

Each engine is one focused effort away from a full point lift. None requires a v2 rewrite; all are additive to what's already built. The product as a whole is at ~8.1 weighted right now — well above the typical self-built DAT prep market, with a clear path to 9+ across the board in the next 6 weeks of authoring + the OChem predict-the-step build-out.
