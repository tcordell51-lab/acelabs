What the codex captured

  • Recurring voice patterns — the "right?" pivot, permission-to-not-get-it openings, "trust your gut" as a trained skill, the reagent-as-personality move ("Naomi is my homie," "Chromium Jones"), physical metaphors (boat-with-a-sail chair, hot-dog-bun meso, subway-seat steric hindrance), and the long-way-then-shortcut discipline.
  • The 6-move problem setup — name the topic / give the ONE thing / "right?"-checked walk / diagnose where the student would have gone wrong / compress to a portable rule / hand it back.
  • 20 named traps with your exact wording — from radical disguised as addition, to "MCAT not MC plus AT," to the Fisher horizontal=wedge flip.
  • Honest gap notes — OChem and GC are ready to author from at scale. PAT subtypes, real QR walks, and most of Bio are too thin in these three sessions; need more transcript material before authentic authoring is possible there.
