# What "perfect" (11/10) means for one Ace Labs topic

A topic is scored 0 to 10 on ten checks, one point each (half point when partly met).
10/10 = every check met. 11/10 = 10/10 AND a person has checked it on the deployed preview on a phone (390 px) and a desktop (1280 px).

1. Learn: a one-screen lesson for the topic (plain words, the DAT facts that get tested, one worked example), one tap from the topic.
2. Whiteboard: a dedicated interactive board for this topic (picture first, at least one tap/drag/slider interaction, ends in a quick question), opens in place.
3. Drill depth: at least 20 DAT-style questions tagged to the topic (PAT: a generator counts; RC: at least 10 passages' worth of that question type).
4. Explanation standard: every drill item in the topic has an answer line, 3 to 5 steps, a trap line for each wrong choice, and a check question.
5. See it: every drill explanation in the topic links to the topic's board.
6. Game: a 60 to 120 second game that includes this topic's content, with score and streak.
7. Quick check: a 5-question check that marks the topic mastered at 4 of 5 or better.
8. Review loop: misses go to Repair and come back on a spaced schedule (about 1, 3 and 7 days).
9. Engagement: answering earns XP, there is a daily streak, the topic shows a mastery ring, and a student can get a first win within 60 seconds of opening the topic.
10. Phone and correctness: no sideways scroll at 390 px, text at least 13 px, tap targets at least 44 px, no JavaScript errors, zero wrong keys, DAT scope only.

Flow every topic follows: Learn -> Whiteboard -> Drill -> Game -> Quick check -> Review (Repair).
