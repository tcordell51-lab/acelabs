## The 10-week cohort walk (units → weeks × tracks)
Aligns with the hub `W[]`. Chemistry alternates GC/OC; PAT+QR pairs a PAT subsection with a QR unit;
RC+Test-Craft threads RC skill + the full-length review labs. Full-lengths (Booster PTs) drive the back half.

| Wk | Chemistry | PAT + QR | RC + Test-Craft |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | GC-1 Atoms/Stoich | PAT-1 Keyholes · QR-1 Ratios/% | Orientation: DAT map, 200-600, weekly system |
| 2 | OC-1 The Language | PAT-2 Top-Front-End · QR-2 Algebra | RC-1 The Method |
| 3 | GC-2 Bonding/VSEPR | PAT-3 Angle Ranking · QR-1/4 | Study-system lab: PT + Ace Log + FL-driven week |
| 4 | **OC-2 Acid-Base CARDIO** | PAT-4 Hole Punch · QR-3 Probability | RC-2 Question types |
| 5 | GC-3 Thermo/Kinetics | PAT-5 Cube Counting · QR-4 Geometry/Trig | **FL-Review Lab #1** (after PT1): 6-line debrief |
| 6 | OC-4 Reactions | PAT-6 Pattern Folding · QR-5 Word problems | RC-3 Pacing under fatigue |
| 7 | GC-4 Equilibrium/Buffers | PAT+QR full-section timing + traps | **FL-Review Lab #2** (PT2): targeted repair |
| 8 | OC-5 Spectroscopy | PAT+QR weak-area repair | Test-day logistics + mental game |
| 9 | GC-5 Solutions/Electrochem | PAT+QR speed + traps | **FL-Review Lab #3** (PT3): closing gaps |
| 10 | OC-3 Stereo + synthesis/review | PAT+QR final review + pacing | Capstone: test-week plan, taper, send-off |

**Anti-fatigue (10wk is past the 6-8wk comfort zone — see [[cohort-market-research]]):** front-load a Week-1
win; mid-cohort reset at Week 5 (first FL-Review Lab = natural milestone); a clear finish-line simulation +
capstone at Week 10; visible weekly deadlines; coach nudges when a student goes quiet.

## Build order (highest yield first)
Batch-build units in this order so the most test-impactful content lands first:
1. **GC-1, GC-4, OC-4, BIO-1, BIO-2, BIO-4** — very-high-yield content anchors.
2. **OC-1, OC-3, OC-5, GC-2, GC-3, QR-1, QR-2, QR-4, QR-5** — high-yield.
3. Re-render **QR-3, PAT-3** to Studio format; build the remaining **PAT-1/2/4/5/6** (each maps to a Booster/Climb drill).
4. **RC-1/2/3** (method units), **GC-5, BIO-3/5/6** — round out the library.

Each unit: Cleo authors → Priya verifies (0 blockers) → render 3 formats → Gus QA → Thomas sign-off → place + commit.
Crash courses = single high-yield units sold à la carte (question-first cut), with the `workbook.pdf` as the handout.
