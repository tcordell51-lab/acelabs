# Ace the DAT — Cohort Discord (setup blueprint)

The room where the cohort lives between sessions. Built to match the 3-track week
(Chemistry / PAT+QR / RC+Test-Craft), the coaches, and the "misses are gold" culture.
Keep it lean — every channel earns its place or gets cut. Target: a student always
knows exactly where to post, and every post gets a coach reply within a day.

Set the server name to **Ace the DAT — Fall Cohort** and paste the hub link
(the `cohort/` page) into the server description so it's one click from anywhere.

---

## Roles

- **Coach** — Thomas, Sarah, Chapman. Can post in announcements, pin, run voice.
- **Cohort** — every enrolled student. Default role on join.
- **@chem** / **@patqr** / **@rc** — optional self-assign ping roles (a reaction in
  `start-here`) so a coach can tag just the students in a track for a live session.

Two tiers is enough. Do not build a permissions maze — the whole point is low friction.

---

## Channels (by category)

### START HERE
- **welcome** (read-only) — one pinned message: what the cohort is, the weekly rhythm
  (three touchpoints — Chemistry, PAT+QR, RC+Test-Craft), and the hub link. Mirror the
  hub's "How the week works" copy so the story is identical in both places.
- **how-it-works** (read-only) — the norms, pinned:
  - Ask anything. A half-formed question beats a silent stuck.
  - Misses are the point. Post what you got wrong — that's the treasure, not the shame.
  - No pace talk. Nobody's "behind." You're on your line; the plan handles the rest.
  - Coaches reply within a day. If it's urgent, say so.
- **introduce-yourself** — name, test date, the section that scares you most. Builds the
  room; also tells coaches where to aim.
- **get-your-access** — how to reach the hub + this week's live-session links. (No codes
  or passwords pinned in plain text — link to the welcome email instead.)

### ANNOUNCEMENTS
- **announcements** (coach-post only) — cohort-wide notes, schedule changes.
- **this-week** (coach-post only) — pin ONE message each Monday: the week number, the
  three sessions with times, and the two module links that just unlocked. This is the
  single source of truth for "what am I doing this week."

### CHEMISTRY  (Chapman + Thomas)
- **gchem-ochem** — questions on the week's chem. Photos of scratch work welcome.
- **cardio-corner** — the acidity-ranking channel specifically (Acids & Bases week and
  after). Students post an anion pair; a coach or peer walks the CARDIO ladder.

### PAT-QR  (Sarah + Thomas)
- **pat** — keyholes, TFE, angles, hole-punch, cubes, folding. Photos encouraged.
- **qr** — probability, algebra, word problems. Post the problem + your attempt, not just
  the problem.

### RC-TEST-CRAFT  (Thomas + Sarah)
- **rc** — passages, method (Search vs Map vs Read), pacing.
- **full-length-debriefs** — after a Booster PT, drop your 6-line debrief here. This is
  where the FL-driven week starts; a coach turns it into your targeted repair.

### THE LAB  (everyone)
- **wins** — "got it" moments, score jumps, a concept that finally clicked. Feeds momentum.
- **stuck-on-this** — the catch-all when you don't know which channel. A coach reroutes.
- **study-hall** (text) — accountability check-ins: "starting a 50-min block on OChem."
- **resources** — coach-dropped trick sheets, recap links, the hub modules.

### VOICE
- **Session Room** — the weekly live sessions run here (or the coach drops a Zoom link in
  `this-week`; pick one and be consistent).
- **Office Hours** — open voice at set times; a coach is present, camera optional.
- **Study Hall** — ambient co-working voice, mic optional. Presence is the point.

---

## Weekly rhythm in the room

- **Monday** — coach pins the week in `this-week` (3 sessions + 2 unlocked modules).
- **Session days** — live session in `Session Room`; recording link dropped in `resources`.
- **After a PT** — student posts the debrief in `full-length-debriefs`; coach replies with
  the targeted-repair move. This is the loop that makes the cohort more than a lecture.
- **Anytime** — questions in the track channels; `wins` whenever something clicks.

---

## What NOT to build (yet)

- No bots, no XP/leveling, no leaderboards — ranking students cuts against the "no pace"
  norm and the encouraging tone.
- No payment/billing talk anywhere in the server (student-visible = zero money language).
- No auto-role gauntlets. Join → Cohort role → they can post. That's it.

Ship v1 with these channels, run one cohort, then cut whatever stays quiet.
