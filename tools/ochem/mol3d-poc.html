Chirality you can turn over in your hand

A chiral carbon with four different groups. Drag to rotate. Flip it to its mirror image and watch the configuration label switch — the two are non-superimposable. The R/S badge is computed from the actual 3D geometry, so it is always correct and flips with the mirror.
