Once the roots are in, the tree.

The 2026 DAT organic section is thirty questions, and the ADA rewrote its specification this year to weigh acid-base reasoning and curved-arrow mechanisms more heavily and rote reaction recall less. That is good news for anyone who owns the roots, because every tier below is those same eighteen ideas applied. Work them in this order.
