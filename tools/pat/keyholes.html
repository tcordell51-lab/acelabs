Outline Only. Get to the 50-50 Fast.

The keyhole is the object's exact silhouette from some direction - nothing more. Internal features are traps: the aperture only cares about the outline. Orient for 5-10 seconds, eliminate down to a 50-50, then win it with your gut plus one dimension check - is that opening too wide, too short, or notched in the wrong place?

Teach · the object and its three true apertures

Every object owns exactly three honest keyholes

Teach inside keyholes first - exposure therapy. The three apertures are just the three views with everything internal erased. If an answer choice is not one of these outlines - in some turn of the object - it is a trap wearing a costume.
Drill · which aperture does it pass through?

Five doors. One exact fit.

The final 50-50 is usually one dimension. Check ONE distinguishing feature - a width, a notch position - against the object. Do not rebuild the whole solid in your head.
