Solid Means Seen. Dashed Means Hidden.

The load-bearing skill of TFE is the solid-vs-dashed decision — not counting lines (the ADA made that trap dead). Build the object, then check three pairings: width = top + front · height = front + end · depth = top + end. Learn the method, walk it with the pen, then drill it against the clock.
