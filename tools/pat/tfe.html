Module 2 · Top - Front - End

Solid Means Seen. Dashed Means Hidden.

Every problem here forces the solid-vs-dashed decision — the load-bearing skill of real TFE. Don't build the 3D object in your head: orient for 5-10 seconds, find one distinguishing feature, get to a 50-50. Width pairs top and front. Height pairs front and end. Depth pairs top and end — the tough one.

Teach: the inspector's three stops

Google Maps. Satellite view = top. Drop the little guy on the street = front. He walks around the corner = end. On the end view never say left-right: end-left is the front, end-right is the back.
