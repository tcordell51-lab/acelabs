Sarah's playbook · white, colored, and dice questions

Never fold it in your head - eliminate with the tricks

WHITE QUESTIONS
Most abundant type. Answer choices first. Pick out a unique shape - anything that is not just a rectangle or triangle (or the only one of its kind; big, small, odd, a trapezoid - something identifiable). Use unique shapes as your identifiers. Start with choice A unless something in B, C, or D is calling you.
COLORED QUESTIONS
Same method: find a unique colored shape as your guide - where you start depends on what you are given. And the clock rule: when a choice is right on everything, click it and move on. You do not have to prove every other choice wrong.
DICE QUESTIONS - THE FIVE RULES
1 - Rule of 6. The 6 is a train track - arrows pointing at two things. Rotate faces with the rule of 90 degrees.
2 - Rule of 2 and 3. The pips are either parallel or a V. Parallel in the net must stay parallel in the answer - never flips.
3 - Top / front / bottom. Decide which face is the top, then read left and right from that orientation. Flip the die upside down and left-right swap.
4 - Three in a row. In a row of three or more, two dice one full die apart never touch in the answer. Works vertically too, and with the rule of 90.
5 - Cut and paste. Cut along the line, reattach at the furthest end: draw the furthest horizontal line you can and paste the section underneath it.
Sarah's line: I really never want you to fold it in your head. We are able to eliminate all the choices with just the tricks.
