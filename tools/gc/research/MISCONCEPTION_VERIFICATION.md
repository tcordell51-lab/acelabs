# Misconception-Inventory Citation Verification

Verification date: 2026-05-07
Method: WebSearch + WebFetch (Anthropic-hosted tools). Where pages were not accessible (PDF binary, 404, paywall) the limitation is noted explicitly. No citations have been fabricated; every DOI / URL below was returned by an actual search.

---

## 1. Chemical Concepts Inventory (CCI) — Mulford & Robinson 2002

**Verdict: CONFIRMED (with minor terminology note)**

- Citation as claimed: Mulford, D. R.; Robinson, W. R. "An Inventory for Alternate Conceptions among First-Semester General Chemistry Students." *J. Chem. Educ.* **2002**, *79* (6), 739. DOI: 10.1021/ed079p739.
- The DOI resolves on ACS Publications: https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/ed079p739 (accessed 2026-05-07).
- The instrument is correctly described as a 22-item multiple-choice inventory (half are two-tiered).
- Note on naming: ACS / ChemEd X / LASSO and the Bretz lab's later psychometric paper (Krause et al., *J. Chem. Educ.* 2013, 10.1021/ed3004353) all use the spelling **"Chemical Concepts Inventory"** (not "Chemistry Concepts Inventory" as Thomas's draft text used). Both refer to the same instrument.

**Article access:** Paywalled at ACS (subscription / institutional). A ResearchGate copy is posted (https://www.researchgate.net/publication/231265980) but its legal status is uncertain.

**Instrument access:**
- The 22 items are NOT freely downloadable. The ChemEd X intro page (https://www.chemedx.org/JCEDLib/QBank/collection/CQandChP/CQs/ConceptsInventory/CCIIntro.html — the URL Thomas's notes referenced) returned 404 on 2026-05-07; the page may have been retired or moved.
- LASSO Education (https://lassoeducation.org/chemical-concepts-inventory/) hosts the CCI but requires login at https://tools.lassoeducation.org/ — instructor account, not a public download.
- CHIRAL (https://chiral.chemedx.org/instrument/chemical-concepts-inventory) describes the instrument and reliability data but does NOT distribute the items.
- Practical answer: the 2002 article's distractors and stems can be reconstructed from the article text + figures (the paper itself reproduces every item), so reading the J. Chem. Educ. PDF effectively gives you the instrument. There is no separate "instrument file" with answer key on the open web.

## 2. "Bonded-Atoms / Particulate Nature" inventory — Luxford & Bretz 2014

**Verdict: CORRECTED**

The instrument and citation exist, but the **name is wrong** in Thomas's draft.

- Correct name: **Bonding Representations Inventory (BRI)**, not "Bonded-Atoms / Particulate Nature" inventory.
- Correct citation: Luxford, C. J.; Bretz, S. L. "Development of the Bonding Representations Inventory To Identify Student Misconceptions about Covalent and Ionic Bonding Representations." *J. Chem. Educ.* **2014**, *91* (3), 312–320.
- Correct DOI: **10.1021/ed400700q** (NOT ed400370p — that DOI was wrong in the source claim). Resolves at https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/ed400700q (accessed 2026-05-07).
- Construct: 23 multiple-choice items (7 single-tier + 8 two-tier pairs). Themes: periodic trends, electrostatic interactions, octet rule, surface features. Not "particulate nature."
- The "particulate nature" line of work is a different Bretz-lab thread (e.g., Stains & Sevian, Williamson, etc.) — separate instruments, not BRI.

**Article access:** Paywalled at ACS. Was an ACS Editors' Choice when published, so the original release was open-access for ~12 months but is now behind the paywall.

**Instrument access:** Same as CCI — the BRI is hosted on LASSO (https://lassoeducation.org/bonding-representations-inventory/) behind instructor login; CHIRAL describes it; no public file.

## 3. "Acid-Base Chemistry Inventory (ABCI)" — McClary & Bretz 2012

**Verdict: CORRECTED**

The 2012 McClary & Bretz instrument exists, but two things in the source claim are wrong: the name and the journal.

- The instrument is commonly referred to as the **ACID** diagnostic (sometimes "Acid Strength Diagnostic"), targeting **organic-chemistry students' acid-strength conceptions**, not general-chem acid–base reactions broadly.
- Correct citation: McClary, L. M.; Bretz, S. L. "Development and Assessment of A Diagnostic Tool to Identify Organic Chemistry Students' Alternative Conceptions Related to Acid Strength." ***International Journal of Science Education*** **2012**, *34* (15), 2317–2341. DOI: 10.1080/09500693.2012.684433. Resolves at https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/09500693.2012.684433 (accessed 2026-05-07). It is **NOT** a *J. Chem. Educ.* paper.
- A related follow-up reliability paper *is* in J. Chem. Educ.: McClary & Bretz, "Students' Understandings of Acid Strength: How Meaningful Is Reliability When Measuring Alternative Conceptions?", *J. Chem. Educ.* **2014/2015**, DOI 10.1021/ed5005195 — but this is the validation/reliability piece, not the instrument-development paper.
- 9-item, multi-tier, multiple-choice diagnostic.

**Article access:** Paywalled at Taylor & Francis.
**Instrument access:** Not freely distributed on the open web. The 2012 paper presents the items in the article body and appendix, but using them legally requires either reading the paywalled article or contacting the authors.

**Important caveat for an item-writing project:** This instrument is *organic-chemistry acid-strength*-focused. It is not a Brønsted/Lewis general-chemistry acid–base inventory. If you want a Gen Chem acid–base inventory, the closer match is Romine et al. 2016, "How Do Undergraduate Students Conceptualize Acid–Base Chemistry?" *Sci. Educ.* 100(6), DOI 10.1002/sce.21240 — a concept progression rather than a fixed-form inventory.

## 4. Newer (2018–2025) general-chem misconception inventories

**Verdict: CONFIRMED that newer instruments exist; none fully supersedes the CCI for breadth.**

Searches in J. Chem. Educ. and CERP via Google/ACS surfaced the following genuinely new instruments since 2018. Each is topic-narrow (one concept area), so collectively they extend rather than replace the CCI.

- **Enthalpy and Entropy in Dissolution and Precipitation Inventory (E2DPI)** — Atieh, York, Muñiz, Allen, Murphy, Stains, *J. Chem. Educ.* **2019**, *96* (9), 1804–1811. DOI: 10.1021/acs.jchemed.9b00186. 28 items. Validated for Gen Chem II / post-organic Gen Chem II / P-Chem. Hosted on LASSO (login).
- **Flame Test Concept Inventory** — Bretz lab, *J. Chem. Educ.* 2018, DOI 10.1021/acs.jchemed.7b00594. Atomic emission focus.
- **Quantum Chemistry Concept Inventory (QCCI)** — Dick-Perez, Luxford, et al. *J. Chem. Educ.* 2016, DOI 10.1021/acs.jchemed.5b00781. P-Chem, not Gen Chem.
- **Three-Tier Chemistry Concept Inventory (TT-CCI)** — preservice teacher version of the CCI; *J. Chem. Educ.* 2025, DOI 10.1021/acs.jchemed.4c01146 (URL confirmed; full bibliographic match would need a paywall fetch to confirm exact author list and pagination).
- **Organic and Biochemistry Readiness Instrument** — Torres, Pulminskas, Abrams, *J. Chem. Educ.* **2024**, *101* (8), 3352–3361. Readiness for OChem/BioChem rather than Gen-Chem misconceptions per se.
- **Water Instrument** — Balabanoff et al., *Chem. Educ. Res. Pract.* (CERP) **2022**. Surfaced in a search summary; the exact DOI was not returned by the searches I ran, so I am noting it as **unverified to DOI level** — confirm before citing.

No single 2018–2025 publication appears to be a "CCI replacement." For a Gen-Chem item-writing project, the CCI (2002) + BRI (2014) + E2DPI (2019) form the de-facto modern misconception triad cited in the field.

## 5. NBME Item-Writing Guide — Paniagua & Swygert (eds.)

**Verdict: CONFIRMED with edition update.**

- The book *Constructing Written Test Questions for the Basic and Clinical Sciences* exists, edited by Miguel A. Paniagua, MD and Kimberly A. Swygert, PhD.
- The **4th edition (2016)** that Thomas's notes referenced is real but **superseded**. The current edition is the **6th edition, published 2020** (some institutional pages frame it as an iteratively updated guide; NBME calls it the "sixth edition"). The 5th edition was an interim release.
- A widely circulated PDF of the 6th edition is hosted by NBME at https://www.nbme.org/sites/default/files/2021-02/NBME_Item%20Writing%20Guide_R_6.pdf (verified to exist 2026-05-07; the PDF is binary, not introspectable in plain text via WebFetch, but the URL returns 200).
- NBME's official landing page is https://www.nbme.org/item-writing-guide (verified 2026-05-07). A free PDF is offered, **but requires an email-form signup**. English, Spanish, and Portuguese versions are offered.
- Distribution permission: NBME's standard front-matter grants permission to copy/distribute provided the document is not modified and use is noncommercial/educational.

**Bottom line:** Free, but mildly gated by an email form on the official site. Unauthenticated direct PDFs (e.g., the OHSU and UConn mirrors) circulate but originate from earlier editions. Use the NBME-hosted current edition.

## 6. ADA / ADEA — DAT-specific item-writing manual

**Verdict: UNVERIFIABLE that one exists; near-certain that none is published.**

- Multiple searches against ada.org, jcnde.ada.org, and adea.org returned **no DAT item-writing manual**. The candidate-facing materials (DAT 2024/2025/2026 Candidate Guides, DAT User Manual, DAT Sample Test Items PDF) are administration-and-content documents, not item-writing guides.
- JCNDE *does* publish item-development guides, but they are scoped to **board examinations**, not the DAT:
  - "JCNDE Test Item Development Guide" (https://jcnde.ada.org/-/media/project/ada-organization/ada/jcnde/files/test_item_dev_guide.pdf)
  - "INBDE Item Development Guide" (https://jcnde.ada.org/-/media/project/ada-organization/ada/jcnde/files/inbde_item_development_guide.pdf)
- These INBDE/NBDE/NBDHE guides are **publicly downloadable PDFs** with no login, and although they target dental boards rather than the DAT, the item-writing principles (single best answer, vignette construction, avoiding negatives, distractor plausibility) transfer almost 1:1 to a DAT prep-question project.
- ADEA's DAT involvement is primarily candidate communications and articulation with dental schools; ADEA does not publish item-writing guidance.
- I did not find an item-writing manual hosted at ada.org/jcnde or adea.org that is DAT-specific. Per the question's prior expectation: no such document.

## 7. Haladyna & Rodriguez (2013), *Developing and Validating Test Items*

**Verdict: CONFIRMED as 1st edition; no 2nd edition exists as of 2026-05-07.**

- Haladyna, T. M.; Rodriguez, M. C. *Developing and Validating Test Items*, 1st ed.; Routledge: New York, **2013**. ISBN 978-0415876049 (hbk) / 978-0415876056 (pbk). Routledge product page: https://www.routledge.com/Developing-and-Validating-Test-Items/Haladyna-Rodriguez/p/book/9780415876056 (accessed 2026-05-07). Taylor & Francis e-book DOI: 10.4324/9780203850381.
- This title is the successor to Haladyna's earlier **single-author** *Developing and Validating Multiple-Choice Test Items* (3rd ed., 2004). Some search summaries conflate the two; the "4th edition" sometimes referenced in secondary sources is **not** a 4th edition of *Developing and Validating Test Items* — it conflates with the earlier multiple-choice-only series. The Haladyna+Rodriguez co-authored *Developing and Validating Test Items* (2013) remains in its **first and only edition** as of this verification.
- A standalone updated multiple-choice volume — Haladyna, *Developing and Validating Multiple-choice Test Items*, **3rd ed.** (Routledge, 2016 pbk reissue) — exists but is the older single-author series, not a successor to the 2013 Haladyna+Rodriguez book.
- Access: paywalled commercial book; ~$60 paperback; library reserves are the practical free path. A preview PDF is hosted at https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781136961984_A23876892/preview-9781136961984_A23876892.pdf.
