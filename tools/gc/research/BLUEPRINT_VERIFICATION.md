## 1. ADA DAT content outline (Gen Chem) — CONFIRMED (with correction on revision history)

**Source fetched:** ADA *2026 DAT Candidate Guide* (PDF), accessed 2026-05-07

**Source fetched:** ADA *DAT User's Guide* (PDF), accessed 2026-05-07, internal date "April 2025"
URL: https://www.ada.org/-/media/project/ada-organization/ada/ada-org/files/education/dat_users_manual.pdf

### Official Gen Chem topic categories (30 items total, no per-topic % published)

The Candidate Guide and User Manual list 13 top-level categories with sub-topics. **No percentage breakdown or per-category item counts are published anywhere by the ADA.** The Survey of Natural Sciences as a whole is fixed at 40 Bio + 30 GC + 30 OC.

1. Stoichiometry and General Concepts (% comp, empirical formulae, balancing eqns, moles/molecular formulas, molar mass, density, calculations from balanced equations)
2. Gases (KMT, Dalton's, Boyle's, Charles's, Ideal Gas Law)
3. Liquids and Solids (IMFs, phase changes, vapor pressure, structures, polarity, properties)
4. Solutions (polarity, colligative/non-colligative properties, forces, concentration calcs)
5. Acids and Bases (pH, strength, Brønsted-Lowry, calculations)
6. Chemical Equilibria (molecular, acid/base, precipitation, calculations, Le Chatelier)
7. Thermodynamics and Thermochemistry (laws, Hess's law, spontaneity, enthalpies/entropies, heat transfer)
8. Chemical Kinetics (rate laws, activation energy, half-life)
9. Oxidation-Reduction Reactions (balancing, oxidation numbers, electrochemical calcs and concepts)
10. Atomic and Molecular Structure (e- config, orbitals, Lewis-Dot, atomic theory, quantum theory, molecular geometry, bonds, sub-atomic particles)
11. Periodic Properties (representative, transition, trends, descriptive)
12. Nuclear Reactions (balancing, binding energy, decay, particles, terminology)
13. Laboratory (basic techniques, equipment, error analysis, safety, data analysis)

### Has the GC content outline been revised since 2020?

**No.** I confirmed the only ADA-published examination-spec revision in the past five years was for **Biology** (effective Jan 21, 2022): "Updated DAT Biology Test Specifications – March 2022." That document, hosted at https://www.ada.org/-/media/project/ada-organization/ada/ada-org/files/education/updated_dat_biology_testspecifications.pdf (PDF metadata: created 2022-03-08), explicitly underlines new Biology topics (genomics, gene expression, epigenetics, viruses, Archaebacteria) but says **nothing about General Chemistry**.

The next revision is **Organic Chemistry, anticipated April 2026** (https://www.ada.org/-/media/project/ada-organization/ada/ada-org/files/education/new_organic_chemistry_examinationspecifications.pdf, "Updated: 08/13/2025"), and the ADA explicitly says it "do[es] not represent major changes to the content … rather updates to topic names that are simpler and more intuitive." Gen Chem is not on the docket.

**Verdict:** The Gen Chem outline is current as listed; per-topic % breakdown does NOT exist in any ADA document; no GC revision since at least 2020.

## 7. 2022 content-outline revision elevating Laboratory — CORRECTED (claim is wrong)

**Source fetched:** ADA "Updated DAT Biology Test Specifications – March 2022"
URL: https://www.ada.org/-/media/project/ada-organization/ada/ada-org/files/education/updated_dat_biology_testspecifications.pdf
PDF metadata: created 2022-03-08, internal "© December 2021 Department of Testing Services."

**The 2022 revision was Biology, not General Chemistry.** Effective Jan 21, 2022, the ADA added (underlined as new in the document): viruses, Archaebacteria, genomics, gene expression, epigenetics — all in **Biology**. The PDF is one page and never mentions General Chemistry.

The Laboratory category in General Chemistry (basic techniques, equipment, error analysis, safety, data analysis) appears in the 2026 Candidate Guide and the April-2025 User Manual identically; no underlines, no "new topic" markers. Searches for any ADA announcement of a GC-specific revision (any year) returned no results.

**Verdict: CORRECTED.** No 2020 or 2022 General Chemistry content-outline revision happened. The 2022 revision was Biology-only. Laboratory has been a standing GC category for years (it appears in archived prep-vendor outlines from at least 2014; the User Manual's 2014/2019/2024 norm tables all use the same item structure). The next ADA blueprint change is **Organic Chemistry, April 2026**, and even that is described by the ADA as cosmetic.
