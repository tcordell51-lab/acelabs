How this mock works

  • Each section has its own clock. When a section ends, by your choice or by the clock, you cannot go back to it, just like test day.
  • Inside a section you can move freely, mark questions, and use the Review screen to jump to anything open or marked.
  • The periodic table is under Exhibit for the whole natural sciences section. The calculator appears only in quantitative reasoning. There is no other reference sheet, the same as test day.
  • The 30-minute break after perceptual ability is optional. Take all of it, part of it, or skip it.
  • Your place is saved as you go. If the page closes, the clock pauses and you can resume from this screen.
  • The report shows raw scores and time per section. It does not convert to a scaled score; it shows you exactly where the points went.

Best on a laptop or tablet, the closest match to the testing-center screen. It also runs on a phone.
