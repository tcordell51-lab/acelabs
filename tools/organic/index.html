The Only Reaction in Organic Chemistry

You are about to learn a hundred reactions. Here is the secret nobody says out loud: they are all the same sentence. Something rich in electrons attacks something poor in electrons. Learn to see rich and poor, and organic chemistry stops being memorization and starts being recognition.

GREEN = ELECTRON-RICH (the attacker)RED = ELECTRON-POOR (the target)

You can see it now.

Every reaction you meet from tonight on, ask the same two questions first: who is rich, and who is poor? The arrows, the products, the mechanisms - they all follow from that.
