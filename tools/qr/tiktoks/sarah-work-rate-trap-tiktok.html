Work-Rate · Wording Trap
Two phrases.
Two setups.
"If together they work N hours" is NOT the same as "they work together FOR N hours." Different problems. Different equations.
— Sarah catches this every week
read the phrase
word order changes the math
"If together
they work 12 hours…"
SUM of their hours = 12. They might NOT be working at the same time. Sam + Joe = 12.
Sam_hours + Joe_hours = 12

(combined total — not simultaneous)
setup: SUM of hours
"They work
together for
12 hours…"
SIMULTANEOUSLY for 12 hours. Both rates apply for the FULL 12. Combined rate × 12 = work done.
(rate_A + rate_B) × 12 = job

(both running at the same time)
setup: SIMULTANEOUS rates
