The real DAT QR section is 40 questions in 45 minutes — about 1.125 min/q for a passing pace and ~1.0 min/q for a 22+ scorer. We compress it so a sim fits a single tutoring session. The pacing math (target line, behind/ahead deltas, guess EV) is identical. The Two-Pass Drill forces you to skip the 5 hardest-tagged questions in pass 1, then return in pass 2 with leftover time.

Calculator Tax (+10s/open)
When ON, the section clock drops 10 seconds every time you open the on-screen calculator. The real DAT calculator is mouse-click only with no trig/log — top scorers train themselves to avoid it. This toggle simulates that mouse-click cost so you build the avoidance habit. OFF by default — flip it on once you're comfortable with the basic flow.
