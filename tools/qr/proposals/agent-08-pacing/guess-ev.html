Why guessing beats blanking — every time.

DAT QR has 5 answer choices. A random guess is 1-in-5 = 0.20 expected raw points. A blank is exactly 0. There is no penalty for wrong. Blanking is strictly worse than guessing on every single question. This calculator shows you the math.

The math behind the bars
solved = 45 − blank − guess = 40 raw points
finish_all = 45 raw × hit_rate (we use 0.82 baseline) = 36.9
blank_strategy = solved + 0 × (blanks) = 40.0
guess_strategy = solved + 0.20 × (blanks + guesses) = 40.0
Each random guess: 1/5 = 0.20 expected raw points. Each blank: 0. Always.

Guessing on 5 instead of blanking is worth +1 raw point.
On the DAT scaling curve that's roughly +1 scaled point at the 18 → 22 raw range. That can be the difference between a 19 and a 20.

What does +1 raw mean in scaled-score terms?

DAT raw-to-scaled is non-linear: 1 raw point ≈ 1 scaled point in the meaningful 17–22 band where most students live. Above 23 the curve gets steeper.
