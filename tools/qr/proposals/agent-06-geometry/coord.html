Two points. One line. Live.

Drag P1 (gold) and P2 (teal). Slope, midpoint, distance, and the slope-intercept equation update as you move. Click anywhere on the plane to drop a third test point — the panel says whether it sits on the line. The DAT trap is forgetting that a vertical line has undefined slope (not zero) and that the midpoint averages, not subtracts.

P1 = gold, P2 = teal. After clicking "Place test point," tap the plane to drop T (orange).
