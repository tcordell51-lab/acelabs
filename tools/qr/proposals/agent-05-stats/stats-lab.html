DAT trap recognition
When the question stem says "one student scored 98" or "except for one outlier", the writer is steering you to median. If they want median to mislead you, they ask for the mean. Read for the outlier signal first.
H

Harmonic vs arithmetic mean

Round trip at two speeds — average speed is harmonic, not arithmetic. The QR trap is huge here.
harm
HM(a, b) = 2ab / (a + b)  ·  AM(a, b) = (a + b) / 2  ·  HM ≤ AM always
Arithmetic mean: 45.0 mph
Harmonic mean (true round-trip avg): 40.0 mph
Trap gap (AM − HM): 5.0 mph
Recognize
"Same distance, two speeds → use harmonic." If the stem mentions equal time at each speed instead of equal distance, the answer flips to arithmetic. Equal-distance is the DAT default.

Discrete-data traps
1. "Mean of the frequencies" vs "weighted mean of scores" — the writer hopes you average 5, 7, 4, 1 instead of using scoreXfreq / N.
2. Median position is (N+1)/2, then walk down the cumulative frequency. Don't median the scores column.
3. Mode is highest bar, not the highest score.
