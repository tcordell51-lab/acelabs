PATTERN PATH · UNITS

Units — cancel them like variables

The trap: dividing when you should multiply (or vice versa) because the conversion factor is upside-down. Always set up so the unit you are leaving is on the bottom of the conversion fraction.

Canonical Trap
U

Pick the setup, not the answer

One sets up so units cancel. The other multiplies the wrong direction.
DAT-style problem
Convert 540 inches to feet. (12 inches = 1 foot)
The trap pattern
Treat units as variables. They have to cancel.

Write the starting quantity (540 in) and the conversion as a fraction. The unit you want to get rid of goes on the bottom of the fraction so it cancels diagonally with the unit on top of the starting quantity.

If your final units do not simplify to what was asked, the conversion fraction is upside-down. Flip it.

Lock-line: Stack units. Cancel diagonally. The unit you are leaving goes on the bottom.

Drill the recognition

3 problems · same trap, different numbers
Convert 5,000 mL to L. (1 L = 1,000 mL)
5,000 mL · (1 L / 1,000 mL) = 5 L. mL cancels, L stays.
Convert 90 km/h to m/s. (1 km = 1,000 m, 1 h = 3,600 s)
90 · 1000 / 3600 = 25. Multiply by 1000 m/km, divide by 3600 s/h.
Convert 4 ft² to in². (12 in = 1 ft)
Square the conversion: (12 in / 1 ft)² = 144 in²/ft². 4 · 144 = 576.
