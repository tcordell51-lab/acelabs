Branch · 01

Innate — fast and dumb.

First line of defense. Recognizes broad patterns (PAMPs), not specific antigens. Active in minutes to hours.

  • No memory — second exposure is no faster
  • Pattern receptors (TLRs, NLRs)
  • Cells: neutrophils, macrophages, NK, mast, basophils, eosinophils, dendritic
  • Soluble: complement, interferons, cytokines
Branch · bridge

Overlap — both arms.

NKT and γδ T cells share features of both. They have T-cell receptors but respond fast and kill without prior sensitization.

  • NKT: TCR + NK receptors; recognizes lipid antigens on CD1d
  • γδ T: non-MHC restricted; first responders at mucosal surfaces
  • Bridge role: rapid, but with TCR specificity
Branch · 02

Adaptive — slow and exact.

Targets specific antigens via clonal selection. Days to mount on first exposure; hours on second exposure (memory).

  • Memory — second exposure is faster + stronger
  • Specific receptors (TCR, BCR)
  • Cells: CD4+ helper, CD8+ cytotoxic, B cell, plasma, T-reg, memory
  • Antibodies: 5 classes (IgG, IgA, IgM, IgE, IgD)
