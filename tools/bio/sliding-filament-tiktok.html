DAT Trap
"ATP contracts."
Wrong direction. ATP causes detachment. The power stroke happens when the head releases ADP+Pi — before new ATP arrives. Without ATP, you get rigor mortis (locked attachment).
Lock it in
Cross-bridge cycle
Calcium exposes binding sites · myosin latches · power stroke shortens · ATP detaches
1. Ca²⁺ binds troponin → tropomyosin moves
2. Myosin head binds exposed actin site
3. Power stroke (ADP + Pi released)
4. ATP binds → head detaches
5. ATP → ADP+Pi cocks the head; repeat
