The learning track · content review, one night at a time

Retold — learn the subject, not just the flashcards.

Most DAT tools throw you straight into questions. Retold does the opposite: Thomas walks you through a whole subject one night at a time, building everything from a single idea — the story before the reps. It is the track for when a topic is new, or when you are rebuilding it from the ground up. Once it clicks, the engines are where you drill it cold.

How Retold fits

Learn it here. Review it in the engines.

Two modes, one arc — the same order a good tutor would take you through.

01 · Learn

Walk the nights

Each night is a short, guided scene in Thomas's voice. You watch the idea move, answer as you go, and the whole subject assembles from one core question.

02 · Earn

Drop into the engine

Every night hands you the matching reps in the deep engine — reaction maps, timed drills, mocks. You unlock practice once the story makes sense, not before.

03 · Prove

Review it cold

When a topic is solid, the engines and mocks are where you sharpen it under time. New or shaky topic — come back to Retold. Solid topic — go drill.
