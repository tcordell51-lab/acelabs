1Trick board

The pit is moles. Grams, particles and liters of gas live in the flesh. You never go flesh to flesh.

  • Grams to moles: divide by molar mass. Moles to grams: multiply.
  • Particles: moles × 6.022 × 1023, and divide to come back.
  • Liters of gas at STP (0 °C, 1 atm): moles × 22.4 L/mol, and divide to come back.

2Whiteboard notes

The same idea on the crash-course board: moles are king. Start with what they give you, get to moles as fast as you can.

  • The highway: grams of A, to moles of A, to moles of B (the coefficients, the "magic sauce"), to grams of B.
  • Diatomic gases: O2 is 32.0, N2 is 28.0, Cl2 is 71. Double the atom.
  • On-ramp: 5.6 L O2 at STP is 5.6 / 22.4 = 0.25 mol, × 32 = 8.0 g.

    5I do, we do, you do

    I do

    36.0 g of water. How many molecules?

    1. First things first, molar mass of water: two hydrogens at 1.0 plus oxygen at 16.0, so 18.0 grams per mole.
    2. Into the pit: 36.0 divided by 18.0 is 2.00 moles.
    3. Out to particles: 2.00 times 6.022 × 1023 is 1.20 × 1024 molecules.

    We do

    44.8 L of N2 at STP. How many grams?

    1. Done for you: into the pit. 44.8 divided by 22.4 is 2.00 moles.
    2. Your turn: out to grams. N2 is diatomic, so what is its molar mass? Multiply.
    Show the finish

    N2 is 28.0 g/mol, so 2.00 x 28.0 = 56.0 g.

    You do

    3.011 × 1023 molecules of NH3 (17.0 g/mol).

    6Trap card

    Trap: jumping from grams straight to particles or liters. Stop at the pit (moles) first: two arrows, never one.

    Example: 22.0 g CO2 to molecules. The trap answer is 22.0 × 6.022 × 1023 = 1.32 × 1025. The real answer goes through 0.500 mol: 3.01 × 1023.

    10Where to go if you miss

    Every wrong answer on this page links back to one of these. If you keep missing for the same reason, redo that piece first.

    • Multiplied by molar mass when going from grams to moles (or the reverse): Trick board
    • Divided the wrong way round (small over big): Walk it through the pit
    • Jumped from grams or liters straight to the answer without moles: Trap card
    • Stopped in the pit and gave moles as the answer: I do, we do, you do
    • Used the wrong exit (22.4 L/mol for grams, or molar mass for liters): Trick board
    • Forgot that O2, N2, H2 are diatomic, or skipped the coefficient step: Whiteboard notes
    • Still fuzzy on why moles are the bridge: Thomas explains it
