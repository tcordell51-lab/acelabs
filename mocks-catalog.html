How to use the catalog. Take Mock #1 (Diagnostic) before any heavy studying — that's your floor reading. Then study to mastery using each engine's modules + spaced-rep cards. Take Mock #2-#3 to confirm the floor lifted. Mocks #4-#5 establish your "standard" baseline. Mock #6-#7 (Hard) drill exam pressure. Mocks #8-#10 (Final) simulate test-week conditions — take one per week in the 3 weeks before your test date.
