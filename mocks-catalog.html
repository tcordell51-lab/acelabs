The mock catalog.

Three Prometric-style practice tests run through a timed proctored interface — the closest rehearsal Ace Labs ships for Sciences + QR. All three are equivalent-difficulty forms drawn from the same banks — fixed, fully independent, with zero question overlap across the three.

3 Prometric tests · partial simulation

140 questions · 135 minutes · 2 timed sections. Sciences (40 Bio + 30 GC + 30 OChem in 90 min) followed by QR (40 Q in 45 min). Question nav panel, mark-for-review, end-of-section review screen, periodic table for Gen Chem, and calculator widget for QR. Each test draws 140 unique problems with zero overlap across the 3 tests — 420 unique problems total drawn from the AceLabs banks.

Honest scope. PAT and Reading Comp are not included — those sections need image and passage banks Ace Labs does not yet ship. The real DAT scheduled 30-minute break comes after PAT, so these tests are high-fidelity practice for the covered sections, not a full-length administration. Ace Labs does not predict DAT scores or percentile ranks — these tests report raw counts, per-section accuracy, and missed-question review only.

The 3 tests · what each one is for

The three tests are equivalent in difficulty — same banks, same difficulty mix, zero shared questions. What changes is when you take each one: Test 1 early to read your floor, Test 2 mid-prep to confirm growth, and Test 3 close to your date as a final simulation.
