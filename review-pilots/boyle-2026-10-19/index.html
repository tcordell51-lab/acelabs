Model assumptions
  • Ideal gas in a sealed cylinder: amount fixed at n = 2.00/(0.082057 × 300) ≈ 0.08124 mol.
  • Temperature fixed at 300 K by the heat bath. Changes are treated as slow (quasi-static) and thermostatted, so compression does not heat the gas in this model.
  • Starting point V = 2.00 L, P = 1.00 atm (absolute), so PV = 2.00 L·atm and P = 2.00/V for 0.50–4.00 L.
  • The 24 dots are schematic, not a molecular-dynamics simulation. Their count, size and speeds never change with volume.
  • Wall-contact marks are illustrative only and are not measured data.
  • The piston height is drawn proportional to volume (fixed cross-section).
