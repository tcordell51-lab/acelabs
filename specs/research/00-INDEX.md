## Convergent themes (cited by 4+ agents)

1. Drag-drop is the wrong primitive everywhere — five tools have publicly removed it
2. Slider-driven real-time response is the gold standard (PhET pattern)
3. Layered depth on a single canvas (basic/advanced toggle, zoom levels)
4. FSRS-6 not SM-2 for spaced repetition
5. Auto-generate cards from missed retrievals
6. No public leaderboards, no mascots
7. Predicted DAT bio sub-score is the killer motivator
8. Wong palette for color encoding (color-blind safe)
9. Direct labels on diagrams, never side legends
10. GSAP is now free for commercial use
11. Astro + Svelte islands when AceLabs migrates
