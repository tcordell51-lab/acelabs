From clips to a posted video

  1. Record every scene at the pace you'd teach it. Re-record any that don't feel right.
  2. Download all — files arrive as <video>-<scene>-<phase>.webm.
  3. Screen-record the animation full-screen (Cmd+Shift+5), or drop the video HTML into your editor as a source.
  4. Layer each audio clip at its t= timestamp — that's the exact playhead time the scene appears in the animation.
  5. (Face mode) Add the face clips as a circle picture-in-picture (~25% height, top-right or bottom-left).
  6. Trim tight, add music low, export 1080×1920 and post.

Pro tip: The scene timestamps come straight from the animation's own timeline, so your voice lands in sync with the visuals with almost no nudging.
