It is okay to feel this way.

A little anxiety before a high-stakes test is normal. It does not mean you are not ready. Here are three things you can do right now.

Step away for five minutes. Close the tab. Drink some water. Sit somewhere quiet. You are allowed to pause.
Look at what you already know. Open the cheat sheets. Find three things on there that you understand cold. That knowledge is real and it is yours.
Do just one thing today. Open the diagnostic. Answer five questions. That is it — the goal is showing up, not perfecting everything.
