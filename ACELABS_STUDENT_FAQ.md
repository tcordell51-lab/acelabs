# Ace Labs · Student FAQ

Honest answers to the questions students ask before signing up. If a question matters to you and isn't covered here, ask your coach directly.

## "What is Ace Labs?"

Ace Labs is the unified DAT prep system from AceTheDAT. It covers the four science sections of the DAT (Quantitative Reasoning, Biology, Gen Chem, Organic Chem) with four standalone tools under one shell. Each tool has its own coach (Sarah for QR, Riya for Bio, Tommy for GC and OChem) and its own pedagogy designed around pattern recognition + anxiety scaffolding + tutor-driven coaching.

Ace Labs **does not** cover PAT or Reading Comprehension. For those you'll need a separate resource (typically a commercial subscription like DATBooster or Bootcamp.com).

## "How is this different from DATBooster or Bootcamp?"

DATBooster and Bootcamp are comprehensive content libraries — big problem banks, video lessons for every topic, multiple practice tests, polished UX. Most pre-dental students should use one of them as their primary content source. They are excellent products.

**Ace Labs isn't trying to replace them.** It's a different layer in the prep stack:

- **Anxiety-aware design.** Every tool has a "stuck" triage button that gives you three options when you freeze (quick wins from skills you've locked, 60-second breath, or skip-and-come-back). Most commercial bootcamps assume you're already calm and confident. Ace Labs assumes you might not be.
- **Pattern + trap framing.** Every module names the *trap* the DAT puts on every question of that type — not just the formula. Recognizing the trap usually matters more than knowing the math.
- **Tutor-driven.** Designed for 1:1 use with a coach. The 6-stage scaffold (pattern → worked → faded → practice → mastery) maps directly to a 75-minute coaching session.
- **Foundation rebuild.** If you fell off math or science years ago, Ace Labs starts you below where bootcamps assume you are.

If you're already at 21+ in every subject with no test anxiety and a strong commercial subscription, Ace Labs is probably not the highest-leverage thing you can buy. If you're in the 17–20 range, struggle with timed practice, or have been "bad at [subject]" since high school, Ace Labs was built for you.

For honest stacking guidance, see [ACELABS_RECOMMENDED_STACK.md](./ACELABS_RECOMMENDED_STACK.md).

## "Do I really need the ADA official practice tests?"

Yes, if you're aiming above 20 in any section.

Commercial bootcamps and Ace Labs are *approximations* of DAT-style content. The American Dental Association publishes 3–4 official DAT practice tests per cycle — these are the only resources guaranteed to match the real DAT's question style, distractor patterns, and difficulty calibration.

Students who score 22 on commercial mocks but 19 on the ADA tests almost always score around 19 on the real DAT. **Trust the official material as your truth source.** Take one early as a real diagnostic; save the others for the final 4 weeks.

## "Is there a free trial?"

Ace Labs is intentionally bundled with coach time, not sold as a standalone subscription. The reason: students who use the tools alone, without a tutor walking them through the patterns, almost always plateau. The tools were designed for the coaching loop. Releasing them as a free standalone would set students up for poor outcomes and we'd rather not.

If you want to see whether Ace Labs would help you, ask your coach for a 30-minute intro session. They'll walk you through the diagnostic and one module so you can feel the scaffold before you commit.

## "Which subjects should I start with?"

Most students don't engage all four science subjects with a coach simultaneously. Recommended sequencing:

| Your situation | Start with |
|---|---|
| QR floor (< 18 on QR practice) | **QR first** — math gaps don't close passively |
| Strong math, weak science | **Bio first** — biggest volume subject |
| All-around floor | **QR + Bio in parallel** (priority) — QR rebuilds floor while Bio handles volume |
| Strong overall, want polish | **The subject your last mock surfaced as weakest** |

OChem usually goes last because its content (reaction maps) is recognition-heavy and doesn't decay as fast as memorization-heavy subjects. Cramming OChem in the final month tends to work; cramming QR doesn't.

## "How long until I see results?"

By the end of week 2, you should feel the difference on familiar problem types. Pattern recognition kicks in fastest — the "lock" framing means you start naming traps within a week.

Score improvement on practice tests usually shows by week 4–6. The slow rollover from "I know the patterns" to "I can solve under time pressure" is normal and is what the strategy modules and timed practice build.

If by week 4 your practice scores haven't moved at all, the plan needs adjusting (or you're not doing the homework — be honest with your coach about which one).

## "Can I use Ace Labs without a tutor?"

You can, but you'll get maybe 60% of the benefit. The tools are built for a coaching loop:

- A tutor narrates worked examples in a way text can't replicate
- A tutor catches the moment you're frozen even when you don't want to admit it
- A tutor decides when to advance and when to repeat — the tool can't read your face
- The "Sarah-mode" edit overlay lets your coach customize content for you specifically

If you absolutely cannot have a coach, Ace Labs is still better than nothing — but be honest with yourself about the ceiling. A solo student using these tools will likely cap at 20–21 in any subject. A student working with a coach can realistically push to 22–24 with the right plan + supplemental resources.

## "How long are sessions?"

75 minutes (1 hour 15 min). One per active subject per week.

Plans by length:
- **4 sessions / 1 month** — pattern crash for students already in the 18–20 range with daily practice commitment
- **8 sessions / 2 months** — default plan for students in the 17–20 range
- **12 sessions / 3 months** — extra support for students with foundation gaps or test anxiety

Each subject gets its own plan. A student doing both QR and Bio with coaches typically sees both Sarah and Riya weekly.

## "What if I have test anxiety?"

Ace Labs was designed with this specifically in mind. Every tool has a "stuck" button (the small "I need a sec" link in the sidebar) that opens a triage menu when you freeze. You can pick:

- Three quick wins from skills you've already mastered (rebuilds flow)
- A 60-second box-breathing exercise (calms the nervous system)
- Move on for now (mark and skip — come back later)

The anxiety-aware design extends throughout: no timer until you're ready, confidence ratings on every problem, gentle pacing, and language that frames being stuck as normal rather than as failure.

For severe test anxiety, Ace Labs alone isn't a substitute for actual care — please talk to a counselor or therapist. But for the everyday "I freeze under pressure" feeling that most pre-dental students wrestle with, the scaffolding here is real and works.

## "Will Ace Labs tell me when I'm ready?"

Each tool has a mock simulator that predicts a DAT subscore for that section. The mock predictions are reasonably accurate — but they're estimates, not guarantees.

For real DAT-readiness signal, you want **both** the tool's mock score AND a recent ADA official practice test score landing in your target range. If both agree, you're probably ready. If they disagree, trust the ADA test.

## "How much will all this cost?"

Ace Labs coaching pricing depends on the plan you choose and your coach. Ask your coach for current rates.

Realistic full-stack budget for a student aiming 21+ across all sections:

- Ace Labs coaching for 2–3 priority subjects: depends on plan length
- One commercial subscription (DATBooster or Bootcamp): ~$300–$500 per cycle
- ADA official practice tests: ~$100 per test × 3–4 tests
- Optional: foundation supplement subscriptions: $0–$50

The honest middle: most students spending serious money on DAT prep budget $1,500–$3,000 total over 12 weeks. Skipping ADA tests to save money is the most common false economy — the tests cost less than your registration fee and they're the only honest predictor.

## "What if I miss a session?"

A missed session pushes that subject's program back by a week. There's no "make up by working harder on your own" — coaching is load-bearing for the pattern + visual stages. Daily homework continues regardless, but new content waits for the next session.

Coaches usually have a small buffer of slots; ask early if you need to reschedule. Don't quietly skip — tell your coach.

## "Can I switch coaches mid-program?"

Probably not unless something specific isn't working. Disrupted continuity is one of the biggest predictors of stalled progress. If you're considering a switch, talk to your current coach first — most "wrong fit" issues are actually wrong-plan issues, and those can be fixed in one conversation.
