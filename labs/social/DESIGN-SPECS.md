# Design spec sheet — AceTheDAT social graphics

> **For Claude Design (Tommy).** These are the graphics to iterate on visually.
> Claude Code's job was only to write this brief. Build each one in Claude Design
> using the brand system below; export native, no watermark.

## Brand system (every graphic, no exceptions)

| Token | Value |
|---|---|
| Forest green (primary) | `#1f3d2e` |
| Brass gold (accent) | `#b8923d` |
| Cream (light surface / text on dark) | `#f3eee1` |
| Deep pine (deep background) | `#15291d` |
| Ink (body text on light) | `#243b30` |
| Display / headline type | Playfair Display |
| Body / UI type | DM Sans |

**Rules:** NO em dashes anywhere (use periods or "and"). Wordmark = "Ace The" in
cream + "DAT" in gold; the DAT is the hero. Fashion-house-minimal, founder-led,
premium. Do not over-produce. No price or payment language on any graphic.

---

## 1. Series bumper / end-card — "DAT in 60 Seconds"

- **Use:** opening lockup and end-card for every video in the series. Also the
  lower-third wordmark for PIP founder episodes (Posts 4, 6).
- **Canvas:** 1080 × 1920 (9:16). Also export a 1080 × 1080 end-card variant.
- **Background:** deep pine `#15291d` with a subtle forest-green gradient.
- **Copy:**
  - Kicker (DM Sans, gold, letter-spaced, uppercase): `DAT IN 60 SECONDS`
  - Wordmark (Playfair): `Ace The` (cream) + `DAT` (gold), large, centered.
  - End-card sub-line (DM Sans, cream 65%): `Founder-led DAT coaching · acethedat.com`
- **Layout:** wordmark optically centered; kicker above it; thin gold hairline
  rule (1px, `#b8923d` at ~40%) under the kicker. Generous margins.
- **Motion (if animated in CapCut):** wordmark fades up over 400ms, gold rule
  draws left to right.

## 2. Score-win graphic (reusable; Post 4 = Kalea)

- **Use:** proof episodes. Fills the frame behind a PIP of Tommy.
- **Canvas:** 1080 × 1920 (9:16).
- **Background:** forest green `#1f3d2e`.
- **Copy / layout:**
  - Big numeric jump (Playfair, cream): `350` → `450` with a gold arrow between.
    Make the arrow and the `450` the visual hero (gold).
  - Label under it (DM Sans, cream 70%): `Real DAT · section by section`
  - Bottom-left safe zone kept clear for the PIP face.
  - AceTheDAT wordmark small, top-left.
- **Reusable:** numbers are the only swap. Keep a template with editable
  `{from}` and `{to}` text layers. (Week 4 second proof: Maddie ~400 → 450.)
- **No** student last names, no payment language.

## 3. Meet-the-team spotlight (reusable; Post 8 = Chapman)

- **Use:** team-introduction posts. Static graphic or PIP backdrop.
- **Canvas:** 1080 × 1920 (9:16); also a 1080 × 1350 feed variant.
- **Background:** deep pine `#15291d`.
- **Copy / layout:**
  - Headshot in a soft-cornered card, upper two-thirds (clean headshot required).
  - Name (Playfair, cream): e.g. `Chapman`
  - Role line (DM Sans, gold): `Bio · Gen Chem · Organic Chem`
  - Score badge (gold pill, pine text): `530 scorer`
  - One-line insight slot (DM Sans, cream 70%) at the bottom for the teaching point.
  - AceTheDAT wordmark small, bottom-center.
- **Reusable:** swap headshot, name, role, score, insight. Build as a template.

## 4. Soft-offer graphic (Post 10)

- **Use:** conversion post. Single still.
- **Canvas:** 1080 × 1920 (9:16); also 1080 × 1080 feed variant.
- **Background:** forest green `#1f3d2e`, fashion-house-minimal, lots of negative space.
- **Copy / layout:**
  - Wordmark (Playfair) top: `Ace The`(cream) `DAT`(gold).
  - Headline (Playfair, cream, centered): `A few coaching spots open for the next test window.`
  - Sub-line (DM Sans, cream 65%): `Founder-led. Roster kept small on purpose.`

- **No** price, no "$", no payment language. "Free strategy call" only.
