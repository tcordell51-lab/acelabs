### Script beat sheet (30–45s)
1. **The fear (0–8s):** keyholes feel impossible because students mentally rotate every single option.
2. **The habit (8–28s):** stop rotating everything. Eliminate by silhouette extremes first — kill the options that are obviously too wide, too narrow, or the wrong height before you rotate anything.
3. **The cadence (28–40s):** rotate only the 1–2 survivors. Practice this elimination order until it is automatic.
4. **Proof tag (40–45s):** "This is the order we drill 1:1 so keyholes stop eating your time."
