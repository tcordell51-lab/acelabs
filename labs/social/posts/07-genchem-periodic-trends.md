### Script beat sheet (30–45s)
1. **The setup (0–6s):** periodic trends should be guaranteed points, but the DAT hides the points in the exceptions.
2. **The base rule (6–18s):** atomic radius shrinks left to right and grows down a group; ionization energy and electronegativity do the opposite. Pin one arrow and the rest follow.
3. **The 600-level trap (18–38s):** ionization energy is NOT a clean climb across a period. It dips twice. Group 2 → 13 (Be > B): you are pulling an easier-to-remove p electron. Group 15 → 16 (N > O): you are breaking a stable half-filled p³ set. Those two dips are exactly where the DAT writes the distractor.
4. **Proof tag (38–45s):** "Lock the arrows AND the two dips and these stop being misses."
