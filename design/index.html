Ace Labs

Design system

One stylesheet (/shared/ace-ui.css), one bar (ace-ui.js), one question (ace-question.js), one progress record (ace-record.js). Every component a page may use is on this page.

Color

Green and orange are used only for right and wrong. Both also carry a text label, so color is never the only signal.

Type

Kicker, 12 px

Heading one, 32 to 44 px

Heading two, 24 to 28 px

Heading three, 19 px

Lede, 19 px. One or two sentences that say what the page is for.

Body, 16 px, DM Sans. Lines stay under about 68 characters so a paragraph is easy to read on a laptop and wraps cleanly on a phone.

Small, 14 px, for captions and counts.

Spacing

Buttons

Link as button

Every button is at least 44 px tall. One primary button per screen.

Inputs

Cards, chips, meter, numbers

Navigation

The bar at the top of this page is the only navigation. The AD mark and the words Ace Labs always go to the hub. On a phone the same nine places open from the Menu button.

Question

Five lettered choices. Pick, check, then the reveal: the answer, why, the trap in the letter picked, the trick, and a second question on the same skill. Keys: A to E or 1 to 5, Enter to check, N for next, R to retry.

This sample is written for this page so that every part of the reveal is visible. Pick a wrong letter to see the trap line.
