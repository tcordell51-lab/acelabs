How the bands work

Building: under 70 percent right, or answers on fewer than 3 days. Close: 70 percent or more across at least 3 days. Test-ready: 85 percent or more across at least 5 days and 100 answers. Each band reads your most recent 150 answers, and any one day counts for at most 40 of them, so a single long night cannot move it. Under 100 answers, the band is an early read.
