How the bands work

Each section has a rating that moves after every answer you give on your own: up after a right answer, down after a miss, more for a hard question you get right or an easy one you miss. Bands read that rating as the chance of getting an average question right. Building: under 70 percent, or answers on fewer than 3 days. Close: 70 percent or more across at least 3 days. Test-ready: 85 percent or more across at least 5 days and 100 answers. Under 100 answers, the band is an early read. Answers given with a lesson or picture open first do not count.
